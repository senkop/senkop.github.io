Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy covers the mobile games Inkwell Sudoku, Merge Tide, Block Quarry, Orbit Dash, Neon Breaker, Tidewood Isle, Wildmere, Skyfire Squadron, Bramble Bastion, Tiny Links, Perfect Split, Vector Drift, Orbit Guard and Don't Fall Off (the "Games"), published by Mostafa Khaled.

Information we collect

We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not sell personal information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Games are free and show ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the Games ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. On iOS, the Games ask for permission before using your advertising identifier. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings at any time. Some ads are optional "reward" videos that you choose to watch.

In-app purchases

The Games offer optional purchases (for example coin packs, a starter pack and "Remove Ads"). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made so we can deliver it.

Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the Games keep game progress, coins, items, best scores and settings on your own device. This data is not sent to us.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

On Android, the Games can sign you in to Google Play Games Services so your progress is backed up and restored on a new device. When you are signed in, your Play Games profile (player ID and gamer name) is used to identify your save, and your game progress is stored in your own Google account using Play Games "Saved Games". We do not receive your email address or other Google account details, and we cannot read your saved data. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can sign out or delete Play Games data from the Google Play Games settings at any time. If you do not sign in, progress stays only on your device and uninstalling a Game deletes it.

Children

The Games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through an ad partner, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
